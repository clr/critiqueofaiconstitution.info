This site is an analytical criticism of [Claude's Constitution](https://www.anthropic.com/constitution). Not because we think Anthropic has done anything wrong. Quite the contrary! We believe Anthropic has given us an unprecedented opportunity by making this document publicly accessible. Anthropic invites us to understand, debate, and perhaps even contribute back to this document. It is incumbent on us, the rest of the world, to engage in good faith and defend the best interests of humanity.
